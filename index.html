YOUR QUICK START

Know the table.

Short rules, friendly games, and virtual chips that stay virtual.

01♦

Blackjack Lounge

Start with two cards. Hit to draw, or stand to keep your total. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count as 1 or 11.

Tip: 21 is the target, not a guarantee.
02♥

Teen Patti

Each player gets three cards. The demo compares hands by category: trail, pure sequence, sequence, colour, pair, then high card.

Tip: A trail is three of a kind.
03♠

High Card Showdown

Every player gets one card. The highest rank wins the round; equal top ranks share the pot. Ace is high.

Tip: Simple rules, quick rematches.
04♣

Five-card Draw

Each player receives five cards. The demo compares standard poker categories from high card through straight flush. Your hand stays private until reveal.

Tip: A straight flush beats four of a kind.
✓

Virtual chips, real boundaries.

Chips are fictional points for this friend-group game. They cannot be purchased, redeemed, transferred for value, or exchanged for real money. No real-money betting is supported.

⌁

About private online rooms

Rooms use PeerJS for browser-to-browser connections when its public signaling service is reachable. Keep this page open while hosting. This prototype is not a secure production server: the host's browser manages the room, and the host's computer must stay online.
